#### ⚙️ Data Notes

- Filed-date window is Jun 24 – Jul 1, 2026; several transactions in this batch have earlier transaction dates (Future PAC transfers date to mid-June; Stapleton and Evans payroll to May 27). Oregon allows up to 30 days between transaction and filing, tightening to 7 days in the final 6 weeks before an election — we are not yet in that window.
- Kotek's $1.65M in single-day committee transfers is unusual in absolute terms but permitted under Oregon's no-limits regime; treat as a signal of donor coordination, not a violation.
- Several notable non-watchlist committees (IP 1PDX2026 at $414K raised; Community Safety Coalition at $294K; Hege for Oregon at $40.9K; Myrna for Oregon at $36.6K) merit consideration for the watchlist, especially Hege given its receipt of funds from Anna Scharf's HD 23 committee.
